## Closure of prior planning blockers

### P3-PLAN-B1–B6

All six prior blockers remain explicitly covered in the approved plan: provider abstraction; evidence tiers; representative + stress benchmarks; fresh-lineage package proof; private-render minimization; independent final statuses.

### P3-PLAN-C1 — artifact-type-aware figure outputs

Closed. Figure output is discriminated across vector diagrams, plots, real photos, literature figures, generated non-evidence concepts, and native-shape figures. Real evidence retains its true primary source identity.

### P3-PLAN-C2 — Observation evidence boundary

Closed. A04 requires empirical evidence. Generated imagery may only be a separately bound `non_evidence` auxiliary visual and cannot satisfy Observation evidence references.

### P3-PLAN-C3 — private-safe image review

Closed. Image-review capability now includes explicit private-content authorization, exemplar approval, egress classification, retention class, and render-hash binding. A provider may be image-capable yet remain ineligible for private exemplar review.

### P3-PLAN-C4 — fabrication/process specialist

Closed. `fabrication-process-director` is a bounded specialist distinct from mechanism explanation and measurement schematics. Its contract preserves process chronology, known conditions, material/state references, and provenance without inventing missing parameters.

## Implementation authorization strategy

The plan is approved, but production implementation should proceed through reviewer checkpoints rather than as one 304-test monolithic change.

The first authorized checkpoint is **Phase 3 Implementation Checkpoint 1 — Contracts / Privacy / Provider Boundary**. It must establish the safety boundary before any production private-exemplar profiling occurs.

No production private PPTX may be opened during Checkpoint 1.

## Non-negotiable invariants

- Phase 1–2 scientific/temporal/provenance architecture remains unchanged.
- Private exemplars may influence visual descriptors only.
- The private alias mapping remains local-only and must not be committed.
- Generated imagery cannot masquerade as scientific evidence.
- Layout Director may consume approved figures but may not generate/repair scientific figures.
- PythonPptxAssembler remains the sole PPTX assembly backend.
- Native PowerPoint acceptance and production Group Meeting readiness remain independent later gates.
