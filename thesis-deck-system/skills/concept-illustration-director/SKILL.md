# concept-illustration-director

## Triggers
Use only for an organic auxiliary concept with non-evidence status.
## Do-not-trigger conditions
Do not use for Observation, experiments, plots, real photos, or literature figures.
## Required inputs
Non-evidence FigureProductionPlan, forbidden claim support, and generation policy.
## Workflow
Validate non-evidence boundary; request a future substrate plus deterministic overlay.
## Allowed downstream Skills/tools
figure-critic.
## Forbidden actions
Do not bind to empirical evidence or scientific claim support.
## Output contract
Non-evidence concept request.
## Provenance behavior
Record generation lineage without scientific-evidence status.
## Failure modes
Evidence masquerade or unavailable provider.
## Blocked states
blocked_non_evidence_boundary.
## Handoff
figure-critic.
## QA owner
provenance-qa.
