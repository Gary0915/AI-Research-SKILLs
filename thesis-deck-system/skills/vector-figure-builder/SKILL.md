# vector-figure-builder

## Triggers
Use for an approved structured vector/native-plan Figure Spec.
## Do-not-trigger conditions
Do not use for raw prose or scientific interpretation.
## Required inputs
Typed spec, governed style tokens, and source/provenance refs.
## Workflow
Compose deterministic future output from declared primitives only.
## Allowed downstream Skills/tools
figure-critic.
## Forbidden actions
Do not invent science, style, evidence, or render before authorization.
## Output contract
Future output manifest and geometry plan.
## Provenance behavior
Bind output to spec hash and route refs.
## Failure modes
Missing style or invalid spec.
## Blocked states
blocked_missing_provenance.
## Handoff
figure-critic.
## QA owner
figure-critic.
