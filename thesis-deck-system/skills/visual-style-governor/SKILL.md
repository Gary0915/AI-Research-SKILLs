# visual-style-governor

## Triggers
Use when a routed figure needs CP3-governed visual tokens.
## Do-not-trigger conditions
Do not use for standalone art creation or material-color invention.
## Required inputs
CP3 visual style profile, required categories, and route purpose.
## Workflow
Resolve recurring/provisional/fallback/unresolved token policy.
## Allowed downstream Skills/tools
Selected specialist director.
## Forbidden actions
Do not promote provisional evidence or invent material semantic colors.
## Output contract
Style-resolution record.
## Provenance behavior
Bind every token to CP3 profile and evidence tier.
## Failure modes
Missing category or unresolved required token.
## Blocked states
blocked_unresolved_style.
## Handoff
Selected specialist director.
## QA owner
provenance-qa.
